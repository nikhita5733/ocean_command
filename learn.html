Short lessons behind the ocean services used in both games.

What is PFZ?

A Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) is an area identified as likely to have good fish concentrations, based on ocean data such as temperature and chlorophyll patterns.

What is Ocean State Forecast?

The Ocean State Forecast (OSF) predicts waves, wind, and currents so operators can plan safer, more efficient trips.

What are Waves?

Waves are wind-driven movements of the sea surface. Wave height is a key input into how risky conditions are for small vessels.

What is Swell?

Swell is a longer, rolling wave pattern often generated far away, which can make seas feel rougher than local wind alone would suggest.

TRY IT IN OCEAN TYCOON

What are Ocean Currents?

Currents are large-scale water movements. A favourable current can save fuel; a strong opposing current can waste it and increase risk.

What is SVAS?

Small Vessel Advisory and Forecast Services (SVAS) issue guidance specifically calibrated to smaller boats, which are more vulnerable to rough conditions.

What is a High Wave Alert?

A High Wave Alert warns that wave heights have crossed a threshold considered unsafe for most small craft in the affected area.

What is Tsunami Early Warning?

A Tsunami Early Warning is issued based on seismic activity, not visible sea conditions — the sea can look calm even when a warning is active.
